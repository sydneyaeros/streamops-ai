FAQ

Common questions.

Which Xero plans does it work with?

StreamOps AI works with all Xero plans — Starter, Standard, and Premium. We connect via the official Xero API, so as long as you have a live Xero organisation, you're good to go.

What invoice formats can you handle?

Any PDF. We don't require specific templates or structured layouts. Our AI is trained to handle the messy, inconsistent formatting that real supplier invoices come in.

What if a line item can't be matched?

We flag it for your review with a confidence score. The bill is still created in Xero — unmatched items are clearly highlighted so you can assign the correct account code in seconds.

How does beta access work?

Join the waitlist and we'll reach out before launch to onboard you personally. Beta participants receive discounted early-adopter pricing, and you'll get direct input into the product roadmap before we open to the public.

Is my data secure?

Yes. Invoice data is processed in transit and not stored after the bill is created. We use the official Xero API with OAuth authentication — we never see your Xero credentials.
