Effective date: May 2026  ·  StreamOps AI (ASIC Registered)  ·  streamopsai.com.au

StreamOps AI ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights in relation to it. By using our website or services, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Email address — when you join our waitlist or contact us
  • Business information — such as your company name and Xero organisation details, when you engage our services
  • Invoice and financial data — PDF invoices you submit for processing through our service
  • Communications — emails or messages you send us

We also collect certain information automatically when you visit our website, including your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and referral source, through standard server logs and analytics tools.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate and deliver our services, including processing invoices and creating Xero bills on your behalf
  • Communicate with you about your account, updates, and our beta program
  • Send you product announcements and relevant information (you can unsubscribe at any time)
  • Improve and develop our products and services
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Invoice and Financial Data

Invoice PDFs you submit are processed solely for the purpose of extracting line item data and creating bills in your connected Xero organisation. Invoice content is not stored after processing is complete and is not used for any other purpose, including training AI models.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure infrastructure. We use industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit (TLS) and access controls. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

We connect to Xero via the official Xero API using OAuth authentication. We never store your Xero login credentials.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services in the operation of our platform:

  • Xero — for bill creation via their official API
  • Supabase — for database infrastructure
  • Anthropic Claude — for AI-powered invoice processing
  • Vercel — for website hosting

Each of these providers has their own privacy policy governing their use of data.

6. Your Rights (Australian Privacy Act)

Under the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you

  • Request deletion of your information, subject to legal obligations
  • Opt out of direct marketing communications at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) if you believe your privacy rights have been breached

7. Cookies

Our website uses minimal cookies for basic analytics and functionality. We do not use advertising cookies or track you across other websites.
